Abandoned but not Forgotten

The machines we left behind still have stories to tell.

Since the 1960s, NASA has left landers, rovers, mirrors, helicopters and probes on the Moon, on Mars and far out in deep space. Some still work. Some went silent long ago. All of them changed what we know about the universe.

Why this hardware still matters

🔦

Some of it still works

Scientists still bounce lasers off mirrors left by Apollo astronauts to measure the Moon's distance to within a few centimetres. The Voyagers still send data back from interstellar space.

🏛️

It's history you can see

NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has photographed the Apollo landing sites from orbit: the lander bases, the rover tracks and even the astronauts' footpaths.

🧪

It teaches future missions

When Apollo 12 astronauts brought back parts of the Surveyor 3 lander, engineers could study how materials hold up after years on the Moon. Lessons like that help design the next generation of explorers.
